## 🎬 SHOT-BY-SHOT BREAKDOWN

### **SECTION 1: HOOK (0-10 seconds)**

**Visual:** Fast montage of different AI tool logos (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Midjourney, etc.) flashing on screen — chaotic, overwhelming

**Voiceover:** (Confident, energetic)
> "You're drowning in AI tools. 100+ options. Which one's right for YOUR job?"

**Text Overlay:**
- "1,000+ AI Tools"
- "Endless Options"
- "No Clear Path"

**Music/Sound:** Upbeat, tech-focused background music starts. Slight "whoosh" sound on each logo flash.

**Duration:** 10 sec

---

### **SECTION 2: THE PROBLEM (10-20 seconds)**

**Visual:** Screen recording showing:
- User googling "best AI tools"
- Scrolling through Reddit threads
- Confused face expression (or actor showing frustration)
- Clock ticking (time wasted)

**Voiceover:**
> "Wasting hours researching. Paying for tools you don't need. Missing the game-changers."

**Text Overlay:**
- "⏰ Hours of research"
- "💰 Wrong subscriptions"
- "❌ Missed opportunities"

**Duration:** 10 sec

---

### **SECTION 3: THE SOLUTION (20-45 seconds)**

**Visual:** App walkthrough — clean, focused screen recordings

**Shot 1 (5 sec):** Toolnaut homepage loading
- Clean, modern UI
- Logo prominent
- "Know exactly which AI tools to use for YOUR job"

**Voiceover:**
> "Meet Toolnaut. The AI tool finder for humans."

- "What do you do?"
- Click: "Software Developer"

**Voiceover:**
> "Tell us your job in 30 seconds."

**Shot 3 (8 sec):** Results page appears
- Shows recommended tools with:
  - Green checkmarks ✅
  - Clear descriptions
  - Pricing tiers
  - Integration recommendations

**Voiceover:**
> "Get a personalized list. Only tools that fit YOU."

**Shot 4 (5 sec):** Clicking into a tool detail page
- Full info: features, pricing, alternatives
- "Why this tool for you?" explanation

**Voiceover:**
> "No guessing. No waste. Just the right tool, right now."

**Shot 5 (5 sec):** Spend Audit feature - shows how much they're paying
- Dashboard with costs
- Red X on unused tools
- Save $ recommendation

**Voiceover:**
> "Plus, cut your AI bill by knowing what you're actually using."

**Text Overlay (persistent):**
- ✅ 1,000+ AI Tools
- ✅ Instant Matching
- ✅ Save Money

**Duration:** 28 sec

### **SECTION 4: CTA (45-60 seconds)**

**Visual:** 
- App hero shot (clean, centered)
- Smooth fade to solid color background (your brand color)
- Website URL appears on screen large and clear
- Subtle animation: button pulse (call-to-action)

**Voiceover:** (Warm, confident)
> "Find YOUR perfect AI tools today. Zero cost to get started."

**Text Overlay (large, white text):**
```
TOOLNAUT.XYZ

✓ Free to use
✓ 30-second quiz
✓ Personalized matches
```

**CTA Button Animation:** "Get Started Now" button pulses 2x

**Music:** Builds to crescendo, then drops slightly for voiceover

**Final Frame:** Logo + URL (3 sec hold)

**Duration:** 15 sec

## 🎨 VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

### **Color Palette**
- **Primary:** Your brand color (teal/blue if modern tech)
- **Accent:** White (clean, trustworthy)
- **Background:** Dark gray (#1a1a1a) or white
- **Highlight:** Green (#00FF41) for checkmarks/positive signals
- **Danger:** Red (#FF4444) for wasted money

### **Typography**
- **Font:** Modern sans-serif (Inter, Montserrat, or Poppins)
- **Size:** Large & bold (readable on mobile)
- **Animation:** Fade-in, slide-in from bottom/sides

### **Transitions**
- Between shots: Smooth fade (0.3s) or slide transition
- Logo reveals: Bounce-in effect
- Text overlays: Fade-in with slight scale animation

### **Branding Elements**
- Toolnaut logo: Top-left corner (small, persistent)
- Website URL: Large, bottom-center for CTA section
- Color bars: Thin accent bars between sections

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Keep it moving:** No shot >5 seconds without a transition
2. **Text hierarchy:** Make URLs HUGE (readable on 4-inch phones)
3. **Color flash:** Quick bright flash between sections (keeps attention)
4. **Voiceover sync:** Pause music during key voiceover moments
5. **Emotion:** Show the pain (frustrated person) then relief (smiling)
6. **CTA clarity:** Website URL should appear 3+ times
7. **Mobile-first:** Always preview on actual phone
8. **Captions:** Add burned-in captions (7+ million watch without sound)
