### **PHASE 3: Audio Mixing (15 minutes)**

**Audio Levels:**
- Voiceover: -3dB (loudest)
- Music: -12dB (background)
- SFX: -6dB (subtle)

**Music Timing:**
- Fade in: 0:00 (0.5s ramp)
- Peak volume: 0:10-0:45
- Fade out: 0:50-1:00 (1s ramp)

#### Text Animations
- Fade in: 0.3 seconds
- Hold: 2-5 seconds
- Fade out: 0.3 seconds
- No zoom/scale (unless emphasis)

#### Graphics
- Logo watermark: Top-left, 40% opacity, full 60 sec
- Checkmark icons: Green (#00FF41), next to text
- Arrow graphics: Between sections
- Button animation: CTA section (pulse 2x)

---

### **PHASE 5: Color & Effects (15 minutes)**

**Color Grade:**
- Brightness: +5
- Contrast: +8
- Saturation: +10 (slightly vivid)
- Highlights: Slight warm tint
- Shadows: Slight cool tint

**Transitions:**
- Between clips: Fade (0.3s)
- Between sections: Slide Right (0.5s)
- Music fades: Linear (smooth)

**Effects:**
- Video clips: None (keep clean)
- Text: Drop shadow for legibility
- Logo: Subtle glow or opacity fade
- CTA button: Pulse animation (2x in last 5 sec)

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR CREATIVE FABRICA

1. **Search broadly:** "tech", "innovation", "business" get more results
2. **Filter by duration:** Look for 5-10 second clips (easier to use)

5. **Music:** Preview FULL track before downloading (60+ seconds needed)
6. **SFX:** Test volume in your editor (may need adjustment)
7. **Organize:** Name files clearly (hook_logo_1.mp4, problem_frustrated.mp4)
